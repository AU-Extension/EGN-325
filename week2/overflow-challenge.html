Overflow and conversion challenge

Convert between bases, negate with two's complement, predict overflow, and read floating point patterns, then watch a step-by-step 3D solution.

How to use this interactive
  1. Each of the ten rounds asks one question about how a computer stores numbers or text. The numbers change every time you play.
  2. Type your answer. When the answer is a bit pattern, you can also click the gray "?" cubes in the 3D register to set each bit.
  3. Press Check. You get up to 10 points per round, with partial credit where it is fair.
  4. Press Play (or Step forward) to watch the worked solution in the 3D register, one step at a time.
  5. After round 10, review the table of your answers and try again with new numbers.
