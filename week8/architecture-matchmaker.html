Architecture matchmaker challenge

Match real workloads to the right kind of processor, then spot the hazards that slow a pipeline down.

4 hazardsPart 2: 10 points each
100points for a perfect run
How to use this challenge
  1. Wynwood Wheels, a fictional delivery startup in Miami, needs the right processor for each job. Part 1 has 6 workloads. Pick the processor (6 points), then pick the reason (4 points). A processor that works but is not the best choice earns part of the points.
  2. You can pick a processor with the buttons or by clicking a chip in the 3D view. Drag to turn the board and compare the core grids.
  3. After each answer, press Play or Step to watch the tasks flow into the cores and see why one design wins.
  4. Part 2 has 4 short RISC-V sequences. Spot the data hazard (10 points each), then step through the pipeline to check your answer.
  5. Your final score is out of 100. Try again shuffles the reasons and answers.
