Computer layers zoom

Pick an everyday action and zoom from the app all the way down to transistors switching, one layer of the computer at a time.

How to use this interactive
  1. Choose an everyday action at the top, such as adding two numbers in a spreadsheet.
  2. Press Play to follow that action from the app down to the transistors, or use Step forward and Step back to go at your own pace.
  3. Orbit the 3D stack and click any layer, or use the ladder in part 2, to jump to a layer.
  4. Read what the same action looks like at that layer and which week of the course covers it.
  5. Try every action, then answer the six "Which layer?" questions at the bottom.
