Build the right computer challenge

Match South Florida buyers to the machine that fits their work, and see in 3D which component makes the difference.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read what the buyer does with a computer every day.
  2. Compare the three machines on the 3D boards and the spec cards. Click a part of a board to read its spec.
  3. Click the machine that fits that work best. The most expensive one is not always right.
  4. Choose the component or reason that decides it (that part lights up on all three boards), then press Check.
  5. Press Play for a step-by-step explanation of the answer.
  6. Go to the next buyer and see your final score after ten buyers.
