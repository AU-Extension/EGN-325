Things to notice

    How to use this interactive
    1. Pick a cache size, a line size, and a pattern of memory reads. The panel shows how each 8-bit address splits into tag, index, and offset.
    2. Press Step to follow one read in slow motion: the address is split, the index picks a line, the tags are compared, and the line turns green (hit) or red (miss). On a miss, a block rises from memory into the line.
    3. Press Play to watch the whole pattern, press it again to pause, and press Step back to undo a read. Drag the 3D board to turn it, and click a layer of the pyramid, a cache line, or a RAM cell to learn about it.
    4. Turn on Predict before you see it to say hit or miss before each read, then watch why.
    5. Change one setting at a time and run the same pattern again. That is how computer architects compare designs.
    6. Finish with the quick check at the bottom.
