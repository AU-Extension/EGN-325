How to use this challenge
  1. Palmetto Freight, a fictional logistics office in Doral, has a small upgrade budget. Each of the 10 rounds is one question from their IT team. Each round is worth 10 points.
  2. Read the scenario, work it out on paper or with a calculator, and pick an answer. You get one try per round.
  3. After you answer, press Play (or Step) under the 3D model to watch the calculation built piece by piece. Drag the model to turn it, and click a block to read what it stands for.
  4. Keep the formula card open while you work. At the end you get a score out of 100 and can try again with the answers in a new order.

Formula card

CPU TIMECPU time = instructions × CPI × clock periodCPI is the average number of clock cycles per instruction. Clock period = 1 / clock rate, so 2 GHz means 0.5 ns per cycle.
AMDAHL'S LAWspeedup = 1 / ((1 − f) + f / s)f is the fraction of the time the upgrade affects, and s is how many times faster that part becomes. The part you do not touch limits the whole job.
AVERAGE MEMORY ACCESS TIME (AMAT)AMAT = hit time + miss rate × miss penaltyMiss penalty is the extra time to fetch from the next level when the cache misses.
