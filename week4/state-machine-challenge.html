How to use this interactive
  1. The 3D view at the top has a tab for each machine. Press Run on any tab for a narrated demo: every step shows the current state, the input, and the next state. The state rail lights the state the machine is in now.
  2. Use Step and Step back to go one input at a time, the speed slider to slow Run down, and the buttons under the caption to send your own inputs.
  3. Part 1 (20 points): complete the turnstile's state table. Each correct transition is worth 5 points.
  4. Part 2 (40 points): five vending machine rounds. Predict the credit left and the sodas dispensed after a sequence of coins, then replay it in 3D.
  5. Part 3 (40 points): four drafts of the Calle Ocho traffic light controller. Preview any row in the 3D intersection and pick the transition that first makes the intersection unsafe. A correct first pick earns 10 points, a correct second pick earns 5.
  6. When every part is done you get a score out of 100 and can try again with new rounds.

    Part 1: complete the turnstile (20 points)

    A subway turnstile at the Brickell station has two states, Locked and Open, and two inputs: coin (a fare is paid) and push (someone pushes the arm). It starts Locked. Choose the next state for each row.

    Part 2: predict the vending machine (40 points)

    A soda at a Little Havana bodega machine costs 50 cents. The machine accepts quarters (Q, 25 cents) and half dollars (H, 50 cents). Its only states are the credit it holds: 0¢ or 25¢. Whenever the credit reaches 50 cents it dispenses a soda, and any extra 25 cents stays as credit. It starts at 0¢.

    Part 3: find the unsafe transition (40 points)

    The controller at Calle Ocho (east and west) and SW 17th Avenue (north and south) changes state when its timer runs out (T) or when a pedestrian presses the crossing button (P). Exactly one row in each draft first puts people in danger: two directions moving at once, WALK while cars still have a green or yellow, or a green that turns red with no yellow.
