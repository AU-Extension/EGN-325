Clock and flip-flop timing visualizer

Draw a data signal, play the clock, and watch a latch, a flip-flop, and a register of 3D bit cells decide when to store a bit.

How to use this interactive
  1. Start on the Timing ribbon tab. The clock (CLK) ticks in the top lane, your data signal D is below it, and the bottom two lanes show what a latch and an edge-triggered flip-flop store.
  2. Tap any D slot (in the 3D ribbon or in the 2D diagram) to draw your own signal, or load a preset such as a glitch while the clock is high.
  3. Press Play to sweep through time one slot at a time. The caption explains what each storage element does at that moment, and every rising clock edge flashes.
  4. Open the Register tab: set the D inputs and the load enable (LE), then press Clock tick and watch the bit cells latch.
  5. Open the Counter tab: tick the 4-bit counter from 0 to 15 and see which bits flip on each tick, then finish with the self-check.

4-bit register with load enable

A register is four D flip-flops that share one clock. Load enable (LE) decides whether a clock tick copies D into Q or keeps the old value.
