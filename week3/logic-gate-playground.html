    How to use this interactive
    1. Flip the input switches A, B, and C, or click a switch chip on the board. A glowing green wire carries a 1, a plain copper wire carries a 0, and a dark wire has no signal yet.
    2. Choose a gate type for each gate card and pick where its inputs come from: a switch or an earlier gate.
    3. Choose which gate drives each output. The truth table fills in every input combination for you, and the highlighted row is the one your switches show now.
    4. Press Run to follow the signals one gate at a time, or Step to go at your own pace. Step back goes back one step, and the speed slider makes Run faster or slower. Turn on Predict before you see it to answer what each gate will output before it lights up.
    5. Load a preset (one AND gate, half adder, multiplexer, full adder) to see a finished circuit, then change one wire and watch which rows of the truth table change.
    6. Finish with the self-check at the bottom of the panel.

    Things to notice

    • A gate answers only when every input it uses has a value. Until then its LED stays dark and the truth table shows a question mark.
    • Signals move one level at a time: a gate can use the switches and the gates above it, never a gate below it.
    • Flipping one switch can change several outputs at once, or none. The highlighted row in the truth table shows which.
    • The half adder and the full adder share one idea: the sum bit comes from XOR and the carry bit from AND.
    • Each extra input doubles the rows of the truth table: 2 inputs give 4 rows, 3 inputs give 8.
