Logic gate playground

Place gates, connect them, flip the inputs, and watch the signals travel through a 3D circuit while the truth table fills in.

How to use this interactive
  1. Flip the input switches A, B, and C. A red wire carries a 1, a gray wire carries a 0.
  2. Choose a gate type for each gate card and pick where its inputs come from: a switch or an earlier gate.
  3. Choose which gate drives each output. The truth table on the right fills in every input combination for you, and the highlighted row is the one your switches show now.
  4. Load a preset (half adder, multiplexer, full adder) to see a finished circuit, then change one wire and watch which rows of the truth table change.
  5. Finish with the self-check at the bottom of the page.

Signal flow in 3D

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap an input pad to flip it, or tap a gate to hear what it is doing. Press Play to follow the signals one gate at a time.
