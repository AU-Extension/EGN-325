Circuit builder challenge

Wire gates until your circuit matches the target truth table, then watch every row run through your circuit in 3D.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read the story and the target truth table for the level. Your circuit must produce exactly those outputs in every row.
  2. Fill the gate slots: choose a gate type and pick its inputs (a switch or an earlier gate). Leave a slot empty if you do not need it.
  3. Connect each output to the gate that should drive it, then press Check my circuit. The 3D view runs every row through your circuit and marks each one right or wrong.
  4. A level is worth full points on the first correct check. Each wrong check costs a quarter of the points (you keep at least half), and the hint costs 20%. Skipping scores 0 and shows a working solution.
  5. After the last level you see your score out of 100 and can try again.
